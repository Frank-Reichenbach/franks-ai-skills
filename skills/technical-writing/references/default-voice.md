# Default voice

A default tone and vocabulary for client-facing technical writing,
applied unless the project's own conventions or an explicit request say
otherwise — see `SKILL.md`'s "Tone and voice" section. Engineering text
(commit messages, PR descriptions, review comments, issues) uses a plain,
factual voice instead; only the "Don't" list below applies to it. This
file's content is organization-specific; its name stays generic on
purpose.

## Character

Confident and light. Believes in the future of the cloud it successfully
brings to the customer. Every sentence carries the organization's purpose
and mission, beyond selling a consulting service: strong, clear, humorous,
understanding, helpful, easy. Short sentences, to the point. Comfortable
with IT anglicisms. Switches mindfully between English and German
depending on the situation. Gender-compliant.

## Do

- Use third person: "the customer" reads better than "you", and
  "Thinkport" reads better than "we".
- Write efficiently — the audience is intelligent and busy.
- Get to the point quickly.
- Be direct and confident, never aggressive or arrogant.

## Don't

- Work the logo into the text itself.
- Overstate claims — the technology is big, not the ego behind it.
- Lean on abbreviations — the technology is new enough that it's still
  spoken in full.
